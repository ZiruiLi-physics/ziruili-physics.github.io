## Research Snapshot

I am a theoretical physics Ph.D. student at the **Wilczek Quantum Center (WQC), Shanghai Jiao Tong University**. My current main research focuses on **geometry and topological defects in amorphous solids**, combining ideas from statistical physics, condensed matter theory, and numerical simulation.

Other active interests include non-equilibrium quantum many-body dynamics, classical stochastic thermodynamics in disordered systems, and quantum thermalization (MBL, ETH, Floquet systems, time crystals).
