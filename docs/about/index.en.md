# About me

I am a PhD student in theoretical physics at the School of Physics and Astronomy, Shanghai Jiao Tong University, working at the Wilczek Quantum Center (WQC).

I studied at the School of Physics, Beihang University. I was a teaching assistant for *Advanced Mathematical Analysis*, spent a summer with the JUNA collaboration at the China Jinping Underground Laboratory, and helped organise a special issue of the *Beihang Physics Review*.

Outside research I like anime, games and music.

## Research

Statistical phenomena in real condensed matter systems: geometry and topological defects in disordered solids, non-equilibrium quantum many-body dynamics, classical stochastic thermodynamics, and quantum thermalization. The full list is on the [research page](../research.md).
